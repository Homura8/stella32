# Getting started with StellaOS

A short tour for someone who has just flashed a device. Nothing here needs a
computer once the firmware is on.

## First minutes

1. **Home.** The first screen shows the time, the date and a grid of apps.
   If there are more apps than fit, the ‹ › buttons turn the page.
2. **Wi-Fi.** Until a network is set up, the card under the apps says
   *Set up Wi-Fi*. Tap it, pick your network and type the password, or choose
   *Phone* to enter it from a phone (join "Stella-Setup" and a page opens).
3. **Time zone.** Once Wi-Fi is up, the card asks you to *Choose your time
   zone*. Tap it and pick the nearest city. The clock and the weather both
   follow it; the weather is for that zone's main city until you pick another
   one in *Weather > Location*.

The back arrow at the top left always returns to the previous screen.
Nothing on the touch screen can break the device; every setting can be changed back.

## The apps

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Clock | The time, with a *Night clock* button: a dim, starry face for the bedside. |
| Weather | Now, the next hours and the next days. Long-press the temperature to refresh. |
| Alarms | Weekday or one-off alarms, with snooze. |
| Timer | Presets and a custom time; rings over whatever app is open. |
| Stopwatch | With laps. |
| Settings | Everything below. |

Two more are off until you want them (*Settings > Home screen*):

| App | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Stats | Live numbers sent from your computer, such as CPU load or a temperature. |
| Calendar | Your next events from an iCal link. |

## Settings, in the order they appear

- **Wi-Fi**: choose a network, or set one up from a phone.
- **Display**: brightness, night dimming, light sensor, *Colours* (fine tuning),
  and *Touch* (test the screen, or calibrate it with four taps).
- **Sound & light**: volume, the status light and its brightness, and automatic dimming from the light sensor.
- **Appearance**: the sky follows the real day; pin it to day or night if you like.
- **Time**: your time zone, and the 24-hour clock.
- **About**: version, updates, memory and what is new.
- **Home screen**: show or hide apps and move them around.
- **Units**: °C or °F, the first day of the week, the order of the date.
- **Screensaver**: when the dim idle face appears (never, at night, always)
  and which one (a clock or a small dashboard).
- **Calendar**: where your events come from.
- **Web page**: a page on your home network for a wallpaper, the calendar link
  and pushing numbers to Stats. It is off until you switch it on, and it shows
  its address and a PIN on the device.

## Good to know

- Everything is saved on the device and survives a restart.
- Alarms and timers ring even if another app is open.
- If something looks wrong, *About* shows the version and the lowest free
  memory, which is what to include in a bug report.
- Updating the firmware keeps your settings. *About > What's new* lists what
  changed after an update.
